How the House Works 🏡

A guide to what the house does by itself, and the few things worth knowing how to drive.

1. House Mode — the brain

The house keeps track of one overall state, and almost everything else follows it. You never normally need to set it — it works itself out from who's home and what's happening.

ModeWhenWhat happens
HomeAt least one of us is in Alarm off, heating comfortable (warmest in the evening)
AwayBoth of us out ~20 minutes and no movement inside Alarm arms fully, heating eases right back, camera alerts become urgent
SleepingLate evening: TVs off, lights off, no movement for 20 min Alarm arms the perimeter for the night, heating drops to the night temperature
Coming home from Away: the moment one of us arrives, the house switches to Home and the alarm disarms itself — you'll hear a welcome-home announcement. Nothing to do.

The House Mode card on the Home tab — Mode and Guest are yours to change; Storm and Maintenance light up by themselves when active.

2. Guests staying over

Flip Guest Mode on (Home tab of the app, the House Mode card) when anyone is staying:

  • The alarm won't fully arm if we both nip out and leave guests in the house.
  • The heating never drops to the "empty house" setting — guests won't be left cold.
  • The night-time arming still happens as normal, so the house is still secure overnight.

Just remember to turn it off when they leave.

3. Heating

Fully automatic, using three set temperatures:

  • 20° — comfort, through the day
  • 20.5° — evening heat, from 7pm
  • 19.5° — night, while Sleeping

It eases right back when nobody's home. It even starts warming when one of us is driving home, and gets up earlier on cold mornings based on the forecast.

Want it warmer right now? Boost.

On the Home tab, the heating card has a Boost temperature slider and a Boost 2 h button — pick a temperature, tap Boost, and it holds for two hours, then goes back to the schedule by itself. Cancel ends it early.

The dial on the hallway thermostat works too: turn it up = a two-hour boost; turn it down = cancel and back to schedule. No need for the Netatmo app — don't use it.

The card's ring shows the current temperature and glows orange while the boiler is actually heating; the little dot is the target.

The heating card — current temperature in the ring (dot = target), status line, boost slider and the Boost / Cancel buttons.

4. Alarm

  • Arms and disarms itself with House Mode — see the table above.
  • Front door NFC tag: a backup — disarming normally happens automatically when you arrive home; tap your phone on the tag if it hasn't.
  • Shed: tap the back-door tag before going to the shed — it stands the shed alarm down for up to two hours and re-arms itself after you're done. It also re-arms itself at 10pm regardless.
  • If it triggers: every light in the house strobes and both our phones get alerts, and it announces on the speakers. Disarm from the app or the front-door tag; the lights go back to how they were.

The alarm panels in the app — House and Shed. Normally you never need them (it all follows House Mode), but they're there for manual arming or disarming.

5. Cameras & the doorbell

  • Phone alerts with pictures: anyone at the front, the doorbell or the back garden sends us both a notification — first text, then the photo, then a moving clip. Tap it to watch the footage.
  • Doorbell: a person at the door also pops their picture up on the kitchen display and the sitting-room TV. If the camera recognises them, it says who.
  • Deliveries: the speakers announce couriers ("DPD has landed…") and the postman.
  • Out the back? Tap the patio door tag and the rear cameras stop alerting while you're gardening or hanging washing. It switches itself off when the patio door is closed again.
  • When the house is Away, camera alerts come through as urgent ⚠️ notifications that bypass quiet settings.
  • While the house is Sleeping: anyone poking around the cars sets off a light show and a warning announcement — it enables itself automatically with Sleeping mode.

6. The talking house — what the speakers tell you

  • Deliveries and the postman (daytime only).
  • Alarm events — arming, disarming, triggered.
  • Bins: a reminder at 7pm on bin night, and at 10pm it checks the camera — if the bin isn't out at the kerb, it says so.
  • Cat alarm: when the bird's cage is outside, tap the cage tag — if a cat shows up on the cameras the house shouts about it. The kitchen TV light glows green while it's on guard.
