1. House Mode — the brain

The house keeps one overall state, and almost everything follows it. You never normally need to set it — it works itself out from who's home and what's happening.

ModeWhenWhat happens
HomeAt least one of us is in Alarm off, heating comfortable (warmest in the evening)
AwayBoth out ~20 min, no movement inside Alarm arms fully, heating eases back, camera alerts become urgent
SleepingLate: TVs & lights off, still 20 min Alarm arms the perimeter, heating drops to the night temperature
Coming home from Away: the moment one of us arrives, the house switches to Home and disarms itself — you'll hear a welcome-home announcement. Nothing to do.

The House Mode card on the Home tab, showing the current mode.

Storm and Maintenance on the card are automatic — they light up by themselves (Storm during a weather warning, Maintenance while Colm is working on things) and need nothing from you.

2. Rooms

Every room has its own hidden tab in the app.

  • Tap a room’s picture on the Home tab to open that room’s page — lights, temperature and anything else that lives in the room.
  • Tap the picture again on the room’s page to come straight back to Home.

The room pictures on the Home tab — tap one to open that room.

3. Guests staying over

Flip Guest Mode on (Home tab, the House Mode card) when anyone is staying:

  • The alarm won't fully arm if we both nip out and leave guests in.
  • The heating never drops to the "empty house" setting — guests won't be left cold.
  • Night-time arming still happens, so the house is still secure overnight.

Just remember to turn it off when they leave.

Guest Mode switched on — the toggle turns green.

4. Heating & boost

Fully automatic, using three set temperatures:

  • 19.5° — night, while Sleeping
  • 20° — comfort, through the day
  • 20.5° — evening heat, from 7pm

It eases right back when nobody's home, starts warming when one of us is driving home, and gets up earlier on cold mornings based on the forecast.

Want it warmer now? Boost.

On the Home tab, the heating card has a Boost temperature slider and a Boost 2 h button — pick a temperature, tap Boost, and it holds for two hours then returns to the schedule. Cancel ends it early. The hallway dial works too: up = a two-hour boost, down = cancel. No need for the Netatmo app — don't use it.

The ring shows the current temperature (dot = target) and glows orange while the boiler is heating.

5. Lights & dimmer switches

Most rooms have a little dimmer switch on the wall for the lights. There are two types around the house — an older square one and a newer rounded one — but they work exactly the same way, with four buttons:

The two types — same four buttons, top to bottom.

ButtonWhat it does
Top — OnTurns the room's lights on. Press it again to cycle through the room's moods (bright, dimmed, night…). Hold for full brightness.
▲ UpBrighter — hold to brighten faster.
▼ DownDimmer — hold to dim faster.
Bottom — OffTurns the room's lights off.

Each dimmer controls the lights in its own room.

Handy to know: the switches are only held to the wall magnetically — you can lift one off its plate and use it like a remote control from the sofa or bed, then just pop it back on the wall when you're done. It works exactly the same off the wall.

6. Alarm

  • Arms and disarms itself with House Mode (see section 1).
  • Front door tag: a backup — disarming normally happens automatically when you arrive; tap your phone on the tag if it hasn't.
  • Shed: tap the back-door tag before going out — it stands the shed alarm down for up to two hours and re-arms after you're done. It also re-arms at 10pm regardless.
  • If it triggers: every light strobes, both phones alert, and it announces on the speakers. Disarm from the app or the front-door tag; the lights return to normal.

The House and Shed panels — normally you never need them (it follows House Mode), but they're there for manual arming or disarming.

7. Cameras & the doorbell

  • Phone alerts with pictures: anyone at the front, doorbell or back garden sends us both a notification — text, then photo, then a moving clip. Tap it to watch the footage.
  • Doorbell: a person also pops up on the kitchen display and the sitting-room TV, and it says who if the camera recognises them.
  • Deliveries: the speakers announce couriers ("DPD has landed…") and the postman.
  • Out the back? Tap the patio door tag and the rear cameras stop alerting while you're gardening — it switches itself off when the patio door is closed again.
  • When Away, camera alerts come through as urgent ⚠️ notifications.
  • While Sleeping, anyone at the cars sets off a light show and a spoken warning — automatic.

8. The talking house

What the speakers tell you:

  • Deliveries and the postman (daytime only).
  • Alarm events — arming, disarming, triggered.
  • Bins: a reminder at 7pm on bin night; at 10pm it checks the camera and says so if the bin isn't out at the kerb.
  • Cat alarm: when the bird's cage is outside, tap the cage tag — if a cat appears the house shouts about it. The kitchen TV light glows green while it's on guard.

The kitchen display — the main voice of the house.

11. The music quiz 🎵

A game for everyone: the sitting room TV plays the first few seconds of a song from our own collection, and you race to name it on your phone.

Open this on your phone (works only on our wifi):

🎧 Open the Intro Quiz

StepWhat to do
1Everyone taps the button above and types their name
2While waiting, pick 3 artists you know from the wall — a song from each player’s picks sneaks into the game (nobody knows which rounds!)
3The game master taps Start — the scoreboard and the music appear on the sitting room TV (it turns on by itself)
4Listen… then tap the right song on your phone — faster answers score more!
5Stuck? Anyone can tap “play a bit more” to hear extra seconds — one go at a time: the button unlocks again once the longer clip finishes
6After each round: the album cover and a proper chunk of the song — it plays in full before the next round can start, so enjoy it
7Half time (after round 5): everyone’s phone shows a music fact to read out to the table, then three quick true-or-false questions — worth +50 points each on the scoreboard!

One phone is the game master — it runs the rounds, and a 🎤 banner on every phone shows who it is. The master changes every game: the final scores screen announces who runs the next one.

Ten rounds a game. Every game counts towards the all-time leaderboard, and the TV now shows it — while everyone’s joining, and again beside the final scores. The bragging rights are permanent (and public). If a clip is rubbish (just clapping, say), tap “🚫 bad clip” and it will never appear again.

Good to know: the start screen also has a “no scoreboard” choice — then the music plays from the sitting room speaker instead of the TV. And if a game gets abandoned mid-way (dinner’s ready), tap “abandon game” at the top of your phone screen — no scores are kept for unfinished games.

12. Playing music around the house 🎶

We play music through the house speakers with Music Assistant — search for a song, artist or playlist and it plays on whichever speakers you choose.

⚠️ Do this first — it’s not like Spotify.
You have to pick a speaker before anything will play. On a normal music app you just press play; here, if you don’t first choose where the sound should come out, nothing happens. Always choose the device first.
StepWhat to do
1Open Music Assistant (in the sidebar)
2Choose where it plays — tap the speaker picker and pick a speaker, or Home Music Group to play everywhere at once. This is the step people forget — without it, nothing plays
3Search for a song, artist or playlist and press play
4Set the volume — for one speaker, or for the whole group

Home Music Group plays the two hi-fi speakers and the computer together, in sync — ideal for music right through the house. (For the computer to join in, its Music Assistant app needs to be open on it.)
